Week 1, Day 3 · Block 1 & Week 2, Day 1 · Block 2 · AI Digital Champion

How to Use This

Real employer, real department, real scenario — no fictional companies here. Each scenario names the specific system or tool involved, so Copilot has something concrete to write real steps around, rather than defaulting to vague filler like ‘log into the system.’ System names are illustrative, built to be consistent and realistic — not confirmed as the organisation's actual software. Draft your guide as if a genuine stranger walked in the door with this exact problem, applying one test at each step — could I actually do this, or would I need to ask a question first? Hand each team the scenario matching their own Day 1 organisation and department.

❯ Scenario Bank — One Per Department

Match each team to their own Day 1 organisation and department. If a team's exact combination isn't listed for some reason, any scenario from the same organisation can be substituted.

Blackburn with Darwen Borough Council

Local Authority · Unitary Authority covering Blackburn and Darwen
Department Scenario Description
Housing A tenant says a repair was booked in three weeks ago but nobody's been out yet, and they want an update today. Check the Repairs Management System for the booking status, contact the relevant repairs team to find out what's happened, and update the tenant with a clear next step.
Customer Services A resident used the council's website chat tool but it couldn't answer their question, and now they want to speak to a person. Check the chat transcript for what they already asked, avoid making them repeat themselves, and route them to the right department.
Adult Social Care A family member has called on behalf of an elderly resident, worried that a scheduled visit didn't happen. Check the visit record in the Adult Social Care Case System, confirm what actually happened, and update the family member with accurate information.
IT & Digital Services The council's online services portal is showing an error for multiple residents trying to log in this morning. Check the system status page to confirm whether it's a known issue, log it in the IT Service Desk system if not, and post an update for the customer services team.

Nybble

IT support, cyber security & AV solutions · Blackburn town centre
Department Scenario Description
Client Support & Helpdesk A client's whole office can't print, and they need it working before a meeting in twenty minutes. Check the print server status in the Remote IT Support Ticketing System, talk the client through the most common fixes, and escalate if it's not resolved within ten minutes.
Cyber Security A client has received a suspicious email and wants to know urgently whether it's safe to open. Check the sender address and links without clicking anything, log it in the Email Security Reporting Tool, and advise the client clearly on what to do next.
AV & Systems Installation A newly installed video conferencing system worked perfectly in testing but won't connect during the client's first real meeting. Check the AV Systems Configuration Panel for any changed settings, talk the client through a quick fix, and note what caused it for next time.
Software Development A client says the website Nybble built for them has a broken contact form, and enquiries aren't coming through. Check the form submissions log in the Website Content Management System, test the form yourself, and identify where the messages are actually going.

Star Academies

Multi-academy trust · Headquartered in Blackburn
Department Scenario Description
Central Education Team A school is asking for the latest version of a shared curriculum resource, but isn't sure which version they currently have. Check the Shared Curriculum Resource Library in SharePoint for the version history, confirm the latest version, and share the correct link with the school.
Estates & Facilities A school has reported a heating fault. Use the Facilities Management System's urgency checklist to work out whether it qualifies for an emergency callout, log the fault with the right priority, and confirm next steps with the school.
HR & Talent (Star Talent Academy) A new teacher starting next week hasn't received their login details yet. Check their record in the Staff Onboarding & IT Access System, confirm their account has been created, and send their login details securely.
IT & Digital Systems A school's parent communication system sent the wrong message to the wrong year group by mistake. Check the Parent Communication Platform's audience settings, send a correction to the right group, and log the incident so it doesn't happen again.

Together Housing

Housing association · Operating across Lancashire and Yorkshire
Department Scenario Description
Repairs & Maintenance A tenant has reported the same repair issue for the third time and wants to know why it hasn't been fixed. Check the repair history in the Repairs & Tenancy Management System, find out why previous visits didn't resolve it, and explain clearly to the tenant what happens next.
Tenancy & Customer Services A tenant is querying a rent statement that doesn't match what they think they've paid. Check their payment history in the Rent Account System, compare it against what they're saying, and explain the discrepancy clearly.
Development (New Homes) A family expecting to move into a new development next month is asking whether it's still on track. Check the build status in the Development & New Homes Tracking System, confirm the handover date, and update the family with accurate information.
IT & Digital Services A tenant can't log into their online account to report a repair and is getting frustrated. Talk them through resetting their password on the Tenant Online Portal, confirm they're back in, and log the issue if it keeps happening.

Brookhouse Aerospace

Aerospace manufacturing · Darwen, Lancashire
Department Scenario Description
Production — Composites & Metallics A component has come off the production line slightly outside spec. Log the component and batch details in the Quality Management System, flag it to the quality team, and make sure it's quarantined until reviewed.
Quality & Compliance A customer is asking for documentation proving a batch of parts meets a specific safety standard. Locate the batch's certification records in the Quality Management System, compile the relevant documents, and send them to the customer.
Engineering & Design A test result has been logged against the wrong component reference number by mistake, and it needs correcting before the batch record is finalised. Find the incorrect entry in the Production & Test Data System, correct the reference number, and confirm the fix with the quality team.
IT & Digital Systems The Production & Test Data System has gone down and nobody can log new results. Check whether it's a full outage or a local issue, set up a temporary way for the team to record results on paper, and log the incident in the IT Service Desk system.
