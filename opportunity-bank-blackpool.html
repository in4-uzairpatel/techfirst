AI Budget Opportunity Bank — Blackpool

10 Everyday AI Opportunities Per Organisation · 5 Token Budget

Purpose

Each team gets an opportunity list built specifically for their Day 1 employer. You only get 5 AI tokens. Argue and decide which 5 opportunities matter most to your organisation!

Prep & Rules

  • Match your team to your Day 1 organisation.
  • You have exactly 5 AI Tokens per team.
  • Discuss, debate, and allocate your 5 tokens below!

Run Sheet

1. Read all points for your employer.
2. Discuss and allocate your 5 AI tokens on the ones that save the most time or avoid the biggest risk.
3. Pair with another team, present your top 2 picks, and defend your choice!
“You can't fix everything at once in a real job either — so what actually goes first, and why?”
