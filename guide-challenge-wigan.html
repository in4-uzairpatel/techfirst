How to Use This

Real employer, real department, real scenario — no fictional companies here. Each scenario names the specific system or tool involved, so Copilot has something concrete to write real steps around, rather than defaulting to vague filler like ‘log into the system.’ System names are illustrative, built to be consistent and realistic — not confirmed as the organisation's actual software. Draft your guide as if a genuine stranger walked in the door with this exact problem, applying one test at each step — could I actually do this, or would I need to ask a question first? Hand each team the scenario matching their own Day 1 organisation and department.

❯ Scenario Bank — One Per Department

Match each team to their own Day 1 organisation and department. If a team's exact combination isn't listed for some reason, any scenario from the same organisation can be substituted.

Heinz — Kraft Heinz, Kitt Green

Food manufacturing · Kitt Green, Wigan
Department Scenario Description
Production Operations A production line has been running slightly slower than usual for two shifts, but nobody's logged why. Check the Production Monitoring Dashboard for the shift's speed data, review the handover notes from the previous shift, and log what you find with the floor supervisor.
Quality & Food Safety A batch has failed a routine quality check. Use the Quality Management System to trace the batch back through each production step, identify which ingredient batch or process stage is the likely cause, and log your findings for the quality manager.
Supply Chain & Logistics A regular ingredient delivery hasn't arrived, and production needs to know within the hour whether to adjust the schedule. Check the Supplier & Inventory Portal for the delivery status, contact the supplier to confirm a new arrival time, and update production planning with your findings.
IT & Digital Systems A shift worker can't log into the Production Monitoring Dashboard and their shift starts in ten minutes. Talk them through a password reset, confirm they're back in, and log the ticket in the IT Service Desk system.

Wigan Council

Local Authority · Unitary Authority covering Wigan
Department Scenario Description
Housing A resident's housing application has been sitting without an update for three weeks, and they've called twice asking what's happening. Look up their case in the Housing Case Management System, check the current stage, and reply to them by email with a clear update.
Customer Services A resident's call about a council tax query has been transferred twice already and they're getting frustrated. Look up their account in the Council Tax & Customer Records System, resolve the query yourself if you can, and only transfer them again if genuinely necessary — explaining clearly why.
Adult Social Care A request for an assessment has come in, but it's not clear from the notes how urgent it actually is. Use the Adult Social Care Case System's urgency checklist to work through the referral details, flag it at the right priority level, and pass it to the duty team with your reasoning noted.
IT & Digital Services Several staff in one department suddenly can't access a shared system that was working yesterday. Check the system status page, confirm whether it's a wider outage or an individual access issue, and log the incident in the IT Service Desk system with next steps.

Agilisys

Digital transformation & AI for local government · Wigan town centre
Department Scenario Description
Client Delivery A council client is due to start using a newly rolled-out system tomorrow, but their account hasn't been set up yet. Check the Client Project Portal for their access request, provision their account with the correct permissions, and confirm to the client by email that they're ready to go.
AI & Product Development A caseworker has flagged that the AI tool suggested something that didn't match the case details, and wants to know why. Log the case reference and the AI's suggestion in the AI Platform Support Ticketing System, check the input data for anything unusual, and escalate to the product team with your findings.
Data & Analytics A council client needs a simple report by the end of the day showing service usage over the last quarter. Pull the relevant data from the Data & Reporting Dashboard, filter it to the right date range, and format it into a short summary to send by email.
Internal IT & Systems A new starter's laptop hasn't been set up properly and they can't access anything on their first day. Work through the IT Service Desk's new-starter checklist, confirm their accounts and permissions are active, and get them logged in.
